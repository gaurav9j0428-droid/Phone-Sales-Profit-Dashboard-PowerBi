# Phone Sales & Profit Dashboard (Power BI)

## Overview
Interactive Power BI dashboards analyzing phone sales and profitability across
24 countries, 6 brands, 6 distributors and 5 operators (2018-2021, 2,998 transactions).

## Tools Used
Power BI, DAX, Excel

## Key KPIs
- Total Sales: 127.2M | Total Cost: 82.8M | Total Profit: 44.4M
- Profit Margin: 34.92%
- Total Quantity Sold: 152K units

## Key Insights
- Tuenti leads operators with 42.0M in sales (33%); Movistar is the smallest at 8.8M.
- Tottus is the top distributor (36.6M, 28.8% of sales).
- Apple (32.1M) and LG (31.8M) lead brands by sales.
- Huawei has the highest profit margin (61.1%); Samsung has the lowest (28.0%).
- Q3 is the strongest quarter (35.7M); 2021 sales reached 50.3M, about 130% above 2020.
- Colombia is the most profitable country (2.5M profit).
